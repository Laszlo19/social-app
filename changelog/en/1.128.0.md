# Version 1.128.0

- **Beta features in one place** – experimental and Witchsky fork toggles now live under a single *Beta features* page in Settings, with a master switch to turn them all on or off.
- **Public Likes tab** – you can now see the Likes tab on other people's profiles, not just your own (opt-in under *Beta features*).
- **Search tidied up** – search now always uses the newer engine, and the advanced filter dialog (authors, mentions, domains, hashtags, dates, media, replies, language) is available from the search screen.
- Upgraded to Bluesky **1.128.0**, with the usual round of fixes and improvements.
